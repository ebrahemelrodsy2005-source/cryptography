Overview

Columnar Transposition Cipher is a transposition technique where plaintext is written row-wise into a grid, then read column by column based on a keyword order.

Algorithm

ENCRYPTION:
1. Choose keyword (ZEBRA)
2. Write plaintext in rows under keyword length
3. Assign numbers to keyword letters (alphabetical order)
4. Read columns in sorted keyword order

DECRYPTION:
1. Recreate grid using keyword length
2. Fill columns according to sorted order
3. Read row by row to recover text

🔐 Worked Example (Encryption)

Plaintext: HELLO WORLD
Keyword: HEMA

Step 1: Determine grid size
Keyword length = 4
Text length = 11 (including space)
Rows = ceil(11 / 4) = 3

Total cells = 3 × 4 = 12

Step 2: Assign alphabetical order to keyword
A → 1
E → 2
H → 3
M → 4

Step 3: Fill grid row-wise

H   E   M   A
3   2   4   1   
H   E   L   L
O   _   W   O
R   L   D   x

Step 4: Read columns in sorted order (A E H M)

A → L O x
E → E   L
H → H O R
M → L W D

Step 5: Combine result

Ciphertext = LOX E L HOR LWD
→ LOXE LHORLWD

🔓 Worked Example (Decryption)

Ciphertext: LOXELHORLWD
Keyword: HEMA

Step 1: Determine grid size
Keyword length = 4
Ciphertext length = 12
Rows = 3
Columns = 4

Total cells = 12
 Padding character = x

Step 2: Sort keyword
A E H M
A → 1
E → 2
H → 3
M → 4

Step 3: Fill columns in sorted order

A → L O x
E → E   L
H → H O R
M → L W D

Step 4: Rebuild grid

H   E   M   A
H   E   L   L
O   _   W   O
R   L   D   x

Step 5: Read row-wise
HELLO WORLD
