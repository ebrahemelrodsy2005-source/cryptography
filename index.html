What is Cryptography?

Cryptography is the practice and study of techniques for secure communication in the presence of adversarial behavior. It transforms readable data (plaintext) into unreadable data (ciphertext) using algorithms and keys, and vice versa.

From ancient Caesar ciphers to modern quantum-resistant algorithms, cryptography has evolved to protect our digital world. This interactive explorer lets you dive into classical ciphers, understand their mechanics, and experiment with encryption and decryption.

Each cipher has its unique mathematical foundation, strengths, and vulnerabilities. Explore, learn, and encrypt!
